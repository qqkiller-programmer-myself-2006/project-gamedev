# 3D and Art Style Guide

## Overview
- **Visual Style:** Anime RPG style with a restricted color palette (Black, Red, White, and their shades).
- **Theme:** Dark fantasy, sleek, high contrast.
- **Characters:**
  - **Arin**
  - **Bram**
  - **Cora**
  - **Dain**
  - **Wren** (The fifth sibling/apprentice)

## Color Palette
- **Black (#000000 to #333333):** Used for outlines, deep shadows, and structural elements.
- **Red (#FF0000 to #880000):** Used for highlights, magic effects, important UI elements, and accents.
- **White (#FFFFFF to #DDDDDD):** Used for base clothing, UI backgrounds (with low opacity or high contrast), and bright highlights.
