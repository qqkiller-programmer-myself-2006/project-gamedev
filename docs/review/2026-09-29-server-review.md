Areas that checked out:
- **Determinism:** the only randomness in `src/match` and `src/core` goes through `GameRng`. Time comes from the injected clock.
  `save_state`/`restore_state` round-trip correctly.
- **Embedded Playtest server:** it binds `127.0.0.1` (`game_server.gd:38`).
- **Transfer rules:** `transfer_gold` rejects amounts ≤ 0, oversized amounts, sending to yourself and bad targets. In multiplayer,
  a client can only act for its own slot (`combat_encounter.gd:104`; Merchant and Rest use the sender's slot).
- **Loadout timing:** `set_loadout` is refused during a Match (`room.gd:170`).
- **Worker:** it checks the secret in constant time, checks the token and caps the body size.
- **Content ids:** every item, status, skill, enemy, recipe, clue, class and `ai_class_order` id exists. The only unmatched
  names, `protect` and `shield_wall`, are skill-effect keys, not statuses, and they predate this change.
- **Story restore:** a save restores the rng state, Energy (stored on the character), clues and `gems_earned`. Status effects,
  cooldowns and the encounter itself are all Combat-scoped and empty at Layer start, so they need no restore.
  Restoring does not award Gems twice within a run.
