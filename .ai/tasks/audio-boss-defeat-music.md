# Task: find and wire dedicated Guardian boss and defeat music

Context: `src/client/ui/sound_bank.gd` has `MUSIC_ASSETS`: "guardian" currently uses `music_guardian.ogg` (MintoDog "Hope", a bright orchestral battle track, only a provisional boss theme) and "defeat" currently reuses the short Kenney lose jingle `sfx/defeat_sting.ogg` (plays once, then silence; `play_music` sets `loop = track != "defeat"` and `_should_skip_music` keeps a finished defeat from replaying). Music hooks are in `src/client/match/match_screen.gd` `refresh()` (phase victory/defeat, boss encounter -> "guardian"). Read `docs/research/audio-sourcing.md` and `assets/audio/CREDITS.md` for licence rules and conventions. Do not commit.

## 1. Find tracks (network enabled)
- Boss: a dark, tense, dramatic fantasy boss-battle loop (orchestral/choir/epic), clearly heavier than the regular battle loop (`music_battle.ogg`, Glizzy "Grizzly Dwarf Battle Loop"). Must loop cleanly (loop-ready or with a clean loop point), 1 to 4 minutes.
- Defeat: a somber, melancholic piece 8 to 40 s that plays once (game over), or a low ambient loop if no good one-shot exists.
Licence: CC0 or CC-BY 4.0 only (no NC, no ND); verify on the item page; skip unclear ones. Prefer OpenGameArt (CC0/CC-BY music), itch.io CC0 packs, Kenney, Freesound (CC0 per item). Already used: Zane Little Glizzy pack, MintoDog Hope, YannZ contemplative pack (CC-BY; its other tracks are fine to reuse, e.g. a sad/pause track for defeat, with the credit already in place). If one of the two has no suitable, clearly licensed candidate, leave it unchanged and say so; do not force a weak choice.

## 2. Add the files
Download to a temp dir OUTSIDE the repo, check duration, loop behaviour and loudness. Convert to OGG Vorbis (ffmpeg, stereo ok for music, quality about q4), loudness-match to the existing music tracks (check their integrated loudness with ffmpeg ebur128 and aim within about 2 LU), keep each file under 6 MB. Save as `assets/audio/music/music_guardian.ogg` (replace Hope only if the new track is clearly better for a boss; otherwise keep Hope and add the new one as `music_boss_dark.ogg` and point "guardian" at the better one) and `assets/audio/music/music_defeat.ogg`. Update `MUSIC_ASSETS` in `sound_bank.gd` ("defeat" -> new file). Keep the one-shot behaviour: if the defeat track is a non-looping one-shot keep `loop = false` for "defeat"; if you chose a loop, change that rule accordingly and keep the replay guard test valid. Keep the `defeat_sting` SFX cue as is. If Hope is no longer used, remove `music_guardian.ogg` and its credits entry; otherwise keep. Update `assets/audio/CREDITS.md` (file, source URL, author, licence, edits; CC-BY credit text verbatim), the in-game credits line in `src/client/title/title_screen.gd` (`_show_credits`) if a new author or licence appears, and `docs/research/audio-sourcing.md` (cue table and gap notes). Run the headless import (`"$GODOT" --headless --path . --import`, GODOT=D:\dev-tools\godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe).
